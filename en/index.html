We Provide Legal and Territorial Security

We prepare Boundary Survey and Demarcation Certificates, which are the basic and fundamental instrument for knowing and clearly establishing the precise boundaries of our properties.

Through them, we verify the consistency between property titles and our actual or potential occupation, ensuring there are no doubts about the exact location of our property rights when undertaking and planning building or infrastructure projects.

Through LAND SURVEYS we determine, modify and verify our properties with the objectives of making subdivisions, planning developments and lot divisions, boundary surveys and demarcations. We also carry out surveys for Horizontal Property (HP), Special Horizontal Property and Real Estate Complexes regimes.

Reach and Technology

Based in the city of Esquel, we carry out land surveying work throughout the Province of Chubut. Thanks to technological advances, software developments and access to satellite imagery, we perform Satellite Cartography work worldwide.

Our specialization covers boundary verification, digitization of existing features on rural properties and index analysis to monitor crops and detect water stress, enabling the planning of infrastructure, internal divisions and resource optimization through thematic maps.
